# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, review ideas on their technical merits, and make participation safe for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Acceptable behavior includes constructive feedback, clear technical disagreement, respect for privacy, and accountability for mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an established organizational channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and reassigned.

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in related public or private forums.
